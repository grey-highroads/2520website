01 / ENGAGEMENTS

Three sprints. Pick the problem.

Each engagement is time-boxed, has a clear deliverable, and ends with something your team can execute against. No open-ended retainers until we've proven the fit.

01

Product Strategy & Roadmap

6–8 WEEKS
You built v1 on instinct and it got you here. Now you need a roadmap your team can rally around and your board can believe in. I audit what you've built, talk to your customers and your team, and deliver a prioritized plan tied to your growth targets.
For founders whose roadmap is still in their head or a spreadsheet nobody trusts.
YOU GETA prioritized product roadmap with strategic rationale, stakeholder alignment, and a 90-day execution plan.
02

Positioning & Messaging

4–6 WEEKS
If your team can't explain what you do in one sentence, your market can't either. I define who you are, who you're not, and how you talk about it. This is the work that makes everything downstream (sales, marketing, hiring) easier.
For companies whose pitch changes depending on who's in the room.
YOU GETA positioning framework, messaging architecture, competitive narrative, and homepage copy direction.
03

Pricing & Packaging

4–6 WEEKS
Your pricing was set two years ago by a founder who guessed. Now it's costing you deals or leaving revenue on the table, and nobody wants to touch it. I run a competitive audit, restructure your packages, and model the impact before you ship it.
For teams that know the pricing is wrong but don't know what right looks like.
YOU GETA pricing and packaging recommendation with competitive analysis, revenue modeling, and a migration plan.

You do not need another strategy deck. You need decisions your team trusts.
